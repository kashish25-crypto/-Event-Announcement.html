🌳🌿 📢 EVENT ANNOUNCEMENT – TREE PLANTATION DRIVE 2025 🌿🌳

📅 Date: 25 November 2025

📍 Location: Green park, Jashpur

🌱💚About the Event

We are excited to announce our 🌳Tree Plantation Drive 2025 🌍, an inspiring green initiative created to spread awareness about environmental protection and sustainability. This event aims to motivate students, volunteers, and community members to join hands in building a cleaner, fresher, and greener future. 🌿💫

Trees are the lifeline of our planet — they give us oxygen, reduce pollution, support wildlife, and make the earth beautiful. 🌏🌺

By planting even a single sapling, you contribute to a healthier tomorrow. 🌱✨

🎯🌍 Objectives of the Event

Our event focuses on promoting environmental love and responsibility. The key objectives include:

  • 🌿 Increasing green cover in the community
  • 🌳 Encouraging eco-friendly habits
  • 💧 Promoting awareness on environmental protection
  • 🌎 Reducing pollution and improving air quality
  • 🤝 Bringing students and residents together for a common cause

🌟🎉 Event Highlights

🌱✨ 🌳 Tree Plantation Activity

Participants will plant various saplings like neem, mango, jamun, tulsi, and medicinal plants.

Experts will guide everyone on proper planting techniques. 🌿👨‍🏫

🎤🌏 Eco Awareness Talk

A short and powerful session on protecting nature, climate change, and sustainable living.

“Save Nature, Save Future” will be the main theme. 🌎💬

🎁💚 Eco-Friendly Goodies

Participants will receive beautiful eco-friendly gifts like:

🎁 Seed pens

🎁 Jute bags

🎁 Seed paper bookmarks

📸🌿 Green Photo Booth

A special nature-themed photo corner will be set up where you can click eco-selfies with your planted saplings. 🌱📷✨

🌏✨ Conclusion

Tree Plantation Drive

The Plantation Drive 2025 is not just an event — it is a powerful step toward a greener planet and a brighter future. 🌎💖

Let’s unite, plant more trees, and protect the earth. Your participation can become a spark for big change! 🌱🔥💚
